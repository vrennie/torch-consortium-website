## DiSrupT RR-TB

DiSrupT RR-TB is a research project in KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa, investigating how genomic information can support the management of rifampicin-resistant tuberculosis (RR-TB).

The project evaluates whole-genome sequencing and its use in informing tuberculosis care, including approaches that can generate sequencing results directly from sputum samples.

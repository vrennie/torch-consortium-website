<section class="vision-intro" aria-labelledby="vision-heading">
  <div class="vision-eyebrow">OUR VISION</div>
  <h2 id="vision-heading">We envision a world free of tuberculosis.</h2>
  <p>People and communities suffering from tuberculosis deserve innovative solutions to reduce the burden of disease. Tuberculosis remains a public health problem despite decades of investment in management and control. Whole-genome sequencing is a new tool that can help improve our understanding of tuberculosis.</p>
</section>

<section class="vision-section vision-priorities" aria-labelledby="priorities-heading">
  <div class="vision-section-head">
    <span class="vision-eyebrow">OUR APPROACH</span>
    <h2 id="priorities-heading">How we achieve our mission</h2>
    <p>Five priorities guide our work.</p>
  </div>
  <ol class="vision-priority-list">
    <li class="vision-priority">
      <span class="vision-priority-number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Training and networking</h3>
        <p>Create opportunities for training in sequencing technologies and support networking of junior scientists interested in ’omics research on tuberculosis in high-burden countries.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li class="vision-priority">
      <span class="vision-priority-number" aria-hidden="true">02</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Transmission and outbreak investigation</h3>
        <p>Enhance our understanding of <em>Mycobacterium tuberculosis</em> transmission dynamics and outbreak investigation.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li class="vision-priority">
      <span class="vision-priority-number" aria-hidden="true">03</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Drug-resistant tuberculosis</h3>
        <p>Decipher the emergence and transmission of drug-resistant tuberculosis.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li class="vision-priority">
      <span class="vision-priority-number" aria-hidden="true">04</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Adaptation and evolution</h3>
        <p>Generate new insights into the adaptation and evolution of <em>Mycobacterium tuberculosis</em> under therapeutic pressure for drug-sensitive or drug-resistant tuberculosis.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
    <li class="vision-priority">
      <span class="vision-priority-number" aria-hidden="true">05</span>
      <div>
        <h3>Research into practice</h3>
        <p>Support and perform high-quality research with potential for translation of results into practice and policy.</p>
      </div>
    </li>
  </ol>
</section>

### Justice Tresor N Ngom

Justice joined Stellenbosch University Division of Molecular Biology and Human Genetics in 2021 as a PhD candidate. His project is based on using the Next-generation Sequencing (NGS) method: WGS to decipher the impact of changes in treatment strategies on the XDR-TB population structure in the Western Cape Province over time. Justice obtained his PhD in 2025 and he is currently a Postdoc.

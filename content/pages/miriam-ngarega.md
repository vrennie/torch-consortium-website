#  Miriam Ngarega

![](/assets/media/c9bed5b9-Miriam.jpg)

Miriam holds a Bachelor's degree in Biochemistry from the University of Nairobi in Kenya. She is now completing her Master's degree in Epidemiology at the University of Antwerp, where she also contributes to the TORCH group’s research projects. She has advanced skills in the formulation of research questions relevant to end users, theoretical design of epidemiological studies, statistical analysis, scientific writing, and communication. She has special interest in causal inference and quantitative bias assessment.

# Informed Consent Form (ICF)

In order to participate in the PARR-TB, patients must give their consent to have their RR-TB treatment to be guided by the MICK-MAGMA platform. Before you administer treatment please ensure that the patient has consented and fill in the form by clicking on the button below:

# Sondes kalboussi

Sondes has two master degrees in medical biology and Bionformatics from the university of Bologna, Italy, with an interest in protein bioinformatics and Next Generation Sequencing. She is a research assistant at the Center for Whole Genome Sequencing of Mycobacterium Tuberculosis providing programming assistance for the team.

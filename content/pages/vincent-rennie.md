#  Vincent Rennie

![](/assets/media/0df4254e-Vincent.png)

Vincent is an interdisciplinary researcher, with a background in Evolutionary Biology from the University of Edinburgh (UK). He completed his PhD on extremophile communities in acidic hot springs and developed a suite of bioinformatics tools to better characterize these communities. He also has a design background.

Vincent is currently the project manager of the MAGMA proof-of-concept project and working with international partners, both academic and commercial, to commercialise the MAGMA technology in order to provide global, equitable access to WGS-guided TB care and prevention.

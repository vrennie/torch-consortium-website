#  Zhiheng Wang

![](/assets/media/6acb4424-Zhiheng.jpg)

Zhiheng received a degree from master program European Master in Health Economics and Management hosted by Erasmus University Rotterdam in 2023 and now is a research assistant at the University of Antwerp. Her research mainly focuses on cost-effectiveness studies in drug-resistant tuberculosis diagnosis strategies and treatments. She also plays a role in cost analysis for the SMARTT trial in the TORCH team.
